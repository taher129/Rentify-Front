What to do in case of a claim?

Follow these steps to report a claim:

For Borrowers

  • Secure and document the accident site.
  • Report damage to the lender immediately.
  • Take photos and record the incident in the return protocol.

    • For Lenders

    • Report damage to Rentify within 2 days.
    • Ensure IBAN details are updated for payout.
    • Cancel upcoming rentals if necessary.
