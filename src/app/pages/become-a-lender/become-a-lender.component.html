The most important facts in brief

What costs can I expect?

We have no registration fees and no recurring fees. The borrower pays a referral fee per rental. This can vary from 7.5% to 20% on a successful rental. You can find out more about our premium model below.

Are my items protected?

Yes! Our experience shows that users handle rented items with care. But accidents happen. That's why Zurich offers insurance coverage for items up to CHF 15,000 and for vehicles during the rental period.

What can I charge for the rental?

You alone decide and determine the rental price. As a rule, between 2-5% of the purchase price per day is a good guideline. Depending on the category and demand, your items can be amortized within a few months.

What services do we provide?

We make your items visible for free on the largest rental platform in Switzerland. All our users who become your potential customers are verified. Our payment system guarantees smooth processing and payment of your rental within 1-3 days.

Rentify Premium & Cashback Model

We want to grow with you, so we've developed a dynamic premium model. Borrowers pay a referral fee on top of your rent, which we use to operate our platform. This fee changes with your status and the revenue you generate per month.

Premium Model

  • New Lender The basic premium for a rental is 20% on the rental price for tenants.
  • Power Lender After 5 successful rentals within 12 months, you benefit from our regressive cashback model:
    • Level 1 From CHF 500 turnover/month: premium drops to 15%
    • Level 2 From CHF 1,000 turnover/month: premium drops to 10%
    • Level 3 From CHF 1,500 turnover/month: premium drops to 7.5%
    • For each additional CHF 100 turnover, you get a reduction of 0.5%

We will pay you back the difference to the basic premium each month in the following month.
